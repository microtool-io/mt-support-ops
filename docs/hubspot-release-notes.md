### Recovery cases to cover before enabling note delivery

- Submit a double-click and concurrent worker claims for one release. Crash before sending, after remote acceptance, and after verification but before saving success. Each release should have one durable delivery record.
- Delay visibility of the note, paginate the association list, and interrupt a page read. Keep delivery uncertain until a complete scan confirms a unique match. Repeated reconciliation must only read.
- Exercise zero matches, multiple matches, edited or deleted notes, and a note moved to another ticket. Surface a review action when the outcome cannot be established. A missing match alone is insufficient reason to repeat a create.
- Test revoked scopes, invalid keys, rotation into a different account, rate limits and timeouts during both create and verification. Keep errors actionable and keep ticket bodies and credentials out of logs.
- Verify that Hold creates no note until release, automatic and manual notes describe the right decision, Infeed Off stops intake, and enabling delivery leaves historical releases alone. Define and test what disabling delivery does to pending and uncertain work.
